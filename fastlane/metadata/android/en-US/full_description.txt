Resource Monitor shows a small movable widget on top of other apps with live
readings of your device: CPU frequency, free RAM, disk space, battery
temperature, battery current, network speed and ping latency, each with
its own history graph.

- Choose which metrics to show, number precision, graph direction and size
- Widget width, graph height and overall scale are adjustable
- Runs as a user-visible foreground service with a persistent notification
  that can stop it at any time - or hold the widget in the red zone
- The app explains the "Display over other apps" permission before asking
  for it, never pretends to be the system or another app, and does not
  intercept taps in other apps

Privacy: everything is measured on your device and stays there. No accounts,
no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Ping sends one ICMP echo per
5 seconds to 8.8.8.8 to measure latency; it carries no personal data.
Full policy: https://kotekompotek.github.io/resource-monitor/privacy-policy.html
